# Code of conduct

We want technical discussion here to be candid, useful, and respectful.

Participants should:

- discuss the work, not the worth of the person doing it;
- give reproducible evidence when reporting a defect;
- welcome questions from people learning the architecture;
- respect privacy and avoid publishing sensitive diagnostic data;
- accept correction and make space for other viewpoints.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption, and
publication of private information are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns to `contact@qaisershah.co.uk`. Reports will be handled
privately and judged on the behaviour and its effect on the project community.
